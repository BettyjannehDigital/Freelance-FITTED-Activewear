# Freelance Content Creation – FITTED Activewear

**Freelance Project | 2025**  
**Client:** FITTED — Activewear Brand  
**Platform:** Instagram Reels (primary) + TikTok  
**Result:** Best performing video on client's Instagram account

---

## Overview

FITTED is an activewear brand focused on inclusive sizing and functional design. The founder approached me with a concept she wanted to bring to life but wasn't sure how to execute — a transitional video showing two women of different builds and heights performing the same workout in the same FITTED outfit.

I took creative control of the editing, structure, and execution, turning the idea into a polished, high-performing content piece that became the brand's best performing Instagram Reel.

---

## The Concept

**Brief from client:** Show two models — different body types, same outfit, same exercises — to demonstrate size inclusivity and product versatility.

**My contribution:**

- Interpreted the brief and developed the full editing concept
- Structured the transitions to sync movement between the two models
- Made all creative decisions around pacing, cuts, and visual flow
- Delivered a final edit without music (exported clean for client flexibility)
- Advised the client on platform strategy — recommending Instagram Reels as the primary platform and flagging TikTok audio risks proactively

---

## Goals

- Showcase FITTED's product range across different body types
- Demonstrate the movement and flexibility of the activewear
- Build brand awareness and reach new audiences
- Highlight size inclusivity in an authentic, visual way

---

## Results

| Platform | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram Reels | Best performing video on the account |
| TikTok | ~2,000 views (decent reach for a no-audio edit) |
| Client relationship | CEO expressed interest in long-term collaboration |

**Note on TikTok:** I advised the client before posting that a soundless edit would be difficult to pair with trending audio and may not perform as strongly with the TikTok algorithm. The Instagram-first strategy was the right call — the Reel significantly outperformed expectations.

---

## Key Skills Demonstrated

- **Video Editing** — Transitional editing syncing two models across matching movements
- **Creative Direction** — Took a vague concept and built it into a structured, executable idea
- **Platform Strategy** — Advised client on where and how to post for best results
- **Client Communication** — Managed expectations, flagged risks, delivered on brief
- **Content Strategy** — Understood the brand's goals and translated them into visual storytelling

## About the Client

FITTED is an activewear brand built around the belief that athletic clothing should work for every body. The brand emphasises movement, functionality, and size inclusivity.
